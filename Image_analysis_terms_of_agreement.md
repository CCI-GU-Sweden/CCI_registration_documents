### General Statements

The goal of this document is to bring up, at an early stage, the issues of acknowledgements, collaboration and intellectual properties in general, in order to minimize the risk of misunderstandings at a later stage. This
document is not meant as a legally binding contract, but rather as a starting point for a discussion and specification of mutual expectations.

### Publications

Publications are very important for us, since they show that our work at the facility generates results. As stated in the CCI User Rules, the User agrees to mention the CCI in the Acknowledgements section for regular use of equipment and normal support. However, significant contributions from the CCI Staff could be acknowledge by co-authorship. The level of contribution and possible co-authorship should be agreed upon before the start of a project, but can be renegotiated at a later point during the project.

#### Publication of results stemming from collaboration

 **Default**: The User will acknowledge the CCI and the source of the analysis tool or data in their publications, and will cite the provider’s relevant publication(s) that describe the analysis tool and its use.  
 **Optional**: Both parties participate in writing the paper, and the resulting publication spells out the contribution of each.  

### Intellectual Property

Negotiations on intellectual property issues, as it pertains to patents and commercialization, fall beyond the scope of the present guidelines. However, potential collaborators should at least acknowledge the types of intellectual property issues that may arise in the course of the collaboration. The CCI Staff agrees on not exposing any sensitive information about a project to outsiders. Users will not distribute analysis tools, developed by the CCI staff, without prior agreement with the CCI. The tool developed will be the property of the CCI, but an agreement on a time during which the CCI will not share it with other users can be made.
If the joint experiments or data analyses result in new information or uses having commercial value, both parties will negotiate shared intellectual property.

#### Intellectual property issues

 **Minimal**: The User does not share any intellectual property related to the existing analysis tool or data.  
 **Default**: If the joint experiments or data analyses result in new information or uses having commercial value, both parties will negotiate shared intellectual property.

### Billing and Structure of Individual Projects

Individual projects are often extensions of a first contact at the CCI. If your task is more complex and we together decide to continue working on it, an Individual Project provides us with the means to proceed. Before initiation we will agree on specification, estimation of required time and delivery dates.

The billing can typically be sub-divided as follows:

| Project Type | Description  |  Notes  |
|--------------|--------------|---------|
| Initial Study                   | Mandatory first step in any project; includes processing tests on example data sets, literature search, and initial report writing | Fixed at 10 hours                      |
| Main Project                    | Execution of the project as agreed upon    | Hours as estimated  |
| Adjustments/Additions           | Additional work requested by the user after project delivery      | Hours as requested                     |

*Image Analysis Support Fees*

- Internal fee: 550 SEK/hour (Academia)
- External fee: 1100 SEK/hour (Industry)

**What is covered in the Initial Study?**

1. Processing and analysis tests on example data sets.
2. Searching for and reading literature on related work.
3. Writing of Initial Report.
4. Discussion with the user before and after writing the Initial Report.

**What does the Initial Report contain?**

1. List of deliverables.
2. Delivery date estimate.
3. Cost estimate, divided into sub-categories when possible.
4. Input data requirements (data format, homogeneity, naming conventions, storage solutions).
5. Proof-of-concept results, when possible.

**What do we need from the user for the Initial Study?**

1. A concise description of the scientific questions, experiments, and desired deliverables.
2. A face-to-face discussion to clarify input and output.
3. Representative example data sets.
